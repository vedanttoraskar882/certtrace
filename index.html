About CertTrace

The job doesn't end when the installation is complete.

CertTrace keeps the installation record connected throughout its full lifecycle — from day of commissioning to years of ongoing compliance.

The Problem

Critical records fragment across disconnected systems.

After an installation is complete, certificates, manufacturer warranties, installer approvals, scheme records, grant documentation and callback information are scattered across entirely different systems — with no single thread connecting them.

Certificates Manufacturer Portals Scheme Records Grant Documents Email Threads Spreadsheets Callback Logs

CertTrace creates one continuous digital installation record that connects every element of the lifecycle — so nothing is lost when it matters most.

One continuous record
Installation details, certificates, grants, warranties, approvals and callbacks — all in one place.
Works alongside your existing tools
CertTrace is not a certificate platform, job-management system or charger monitor. It complements what you already use.
Searchable years later
Find any installation by address — engineer, brand, warranty status and full callback history instantly accessible.

Spotlight

The Warranty Rule Engine

Each manufacturer has its own warranty rules — and they change over time. The Warranty Rule Engine evaluates the exact rule set applicable at the point of commissioning, so historical installations are never affected by later rule changes.

Versioned by design. Historical installations retain the rule version that was applicable when they were commissioned — ensuring the warranty assessment remains accurate and auditable years later.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Clear answers. No ambiguity.

CertTrace is a compliance and warranty lifecycle platform for UK electrical contractors. It creates one continuous digital installation record covering installation details, certificates, grant documentation, manufacturer warranties, approvals, compliance requirements, standards changes, callback history and homeowner verification — connected throughout the full lifecycle of each installation.
CertTrace is designed for UK electrical contractors — sole traders through to multi-engineer businesses and group operations. The initial focus is on EV chargepoint installers, where the complexity of manufacturer warranties, scheme registrations and grant documentation is particularly acute.
No. CertTrace is not a certificate production platform. It stores and links certificates — but it does not generate them. It is designed to work alongside your existing certificate software, not replace it.
No. CertTrace does not handle quoting, scheduling, invoicing or job management. It is focused on the compliance and warranty lifecycle record that begins at the point of installation and continues indefinitely. It works alongside existing job-management tools.
No. CertTrace is not a charger monitoring or IoT platform. It does not connect to chargepoints or read live operational data. It manages the compliance and warranty lifecycle record — not real-time device telemetry.
The Warranty Rule Engine evaluates manufacturer-specific rules for each installation — including brand, model, warranty duration, commissioning date, registration requirements, installer approval status and any exceptions or downgrade conditions. Crucially, the rule version applicable at the time of commissioning is retained with the record — so historical installations are never affected by later rule changes.
Yes. The Homeowner Verification Portal gives homeowners controlled access to their relevant installation information — including certificate details, warranty status and installer identity. An optional white-label version is available as an add-on for contractors who want to present it under their own brand.
Initially, yes. CertTrace is built first for UK electrical contractors installing EV chargepoints, where the warranty, scheme and compliance landscape is particularly complex. The platform is designed to expand across electrical installation types over time.
CertTrace is priced on a flat monthly subscription per business — not per user. Plans start at £34/month for sole traders, £89/month for small teams (2–5 engineers), £229/month for multi-engineer businesses (6–20 engineers), and £495/month for groups (21+ engineers). Optional add-ons include the White-label Homeowner Portal (£45/month) and Assisted Migration (£295 one-off).
No. Pricing is per business. Each plan tier is defined by the number of engineers in the business — but you pay one monthly subscription for the business, not a separate fee per individual user account.
Yes. The Assisted Migration add-on (£295 one-off) covers the service to migrate historical installation records into CertTrace. This is available as an optional add-on for businesses who want their existing portfolio brought into the platform.
CertTrace is designed for UK cloud hosting and data residency. The platform includes role-based access control, an append-only audit trail and secure document storage.
